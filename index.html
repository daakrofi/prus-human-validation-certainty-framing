How do users speculate about unreleased products?

Review 500 individual sentences from pre-release Steam discussions and identify three simple components: uncertainty, a possible idea, and its product topic.

Before you begin

Identify three components

We are studying when users speculate about unreleased products. You will see one sentence at a time. Work through the following steps using only the sentence shown.

  1. 1

    Uncertainty cue or framing

    Decide if the sentence signals uncertainty through words such as maybe, might, I think, or through a question, prediction, hypothetical, or other uncertain framing.

    A question is also an expression of uncertainty. If it only asks for information and does not suggest a possible answer, use “Yes, but it is just a request for info.”

  2. 2

    Uncertain proposition

    Decide if the uncertainty applies to a possible claim, explanation, prediction, interpretation, theory, or future product state.

  3. 3

    Product topic domain

    Select every domain that the possible idea concerns.

    Content
    What the game contains or offers, such as story, features, characters, mechanics, or modes.
    Performance
    How the game functions, such as bugs, stability, framerate, servers, or optimisation.
    Requirements & access
    What is needed to obtain or use the game, such as hardware, price, platform, region, or release access.
