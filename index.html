How do users speculate about unreleased products?

Review 500 individual sentences from pre-release Steam discussions and identify uncertainty, a proposition or product-experience concern, and its product topic.

Before you begin

Identify four components

We are studying Pre-Release User Speculation (PRUS): when online users post possible ideas about an unreleased product without presenting those ideas as settled knowledge. This can help online communities make sense of incomplete product information. For this exercise, the posts are about video games. You will see one sentence at a time. Work through the following steps using only the sentence shown.

  1. 1

    Certainty scale

    Rate how certain the user seems, from 1 (very uncertain) to 5 (very certain).

    If the sentence only asks for information and does not suggest a possible answer, use “This is purely a request for information.”

  2. 2

    Game relation

    Decide whether the user's uncertainty is related to the game.

  3. 3

    Proposition or product-experience concern

    Decide whether the post includes a claim, explanation, prediction, interpretation, theory, concern, challenge, or mention of the future state of the game.

  4. 4

    Product topic domain

    Select every domain that the user's proposition or product-experience concern is about.

    Content
    What the game contains or offers, such as story, features, characters, mechanics, or modes.
    Performance
    How the game functions, such as bugs, stability, framerate, servers, or optimisation.
    Requirements & access
    What is needed to obtain or use the game, such as hardware, price, platform, region, or release access.
